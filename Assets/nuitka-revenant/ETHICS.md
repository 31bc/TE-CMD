# Responsible-use policy

REVENANT is published for legitimate reverse engineering, interoperability research, defensive security, malware analysis, education and recovery of software you own.

Acceptable uses include:

- analyzing your own binaries or software you have explicit permission to inspect;
- investigating malware or suspicious files in a controlled defensive environment;
- security auditing performed under a clear authorization agreement;
- CTFs, training labs and research samples where reverse engineering is expected;
- studying Nuitka and compiler behavior with redistributable test programs.

Do not use this project to:

- steal or republish proprietary source code;
- bypass licensing, DRM, access controls or paid functionality;
- extract credentials or secrets for unauthorized access;
- analyze a third-party target when you do not have a lawful basis or permission;
- violate applicable law, contracts, licenses or platform terms.

The ability to inspect a binary does not grant the right to use or redistribute what it contains. Contributors must not upload proprietary samples, credentials, personal data or commercial source code to issues or pull requests.

When authorization is unclear, do not analyze the target.
